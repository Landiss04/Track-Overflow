# brakes-apply-together

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Train Controller
**Provenance:** asserted by Kevin Schillinger 2026-10-17
**Aliases:** both brakes, service and emergency brake together, simultaneous braking, brake precedence
**Last updated:** 2026-10-17

## Definition

Neither brake supersedes the other. Nothing prevents the service brake and the
emergency brake from being applied at the same time.

## Notes

- Covers [service-brake-command](../signals/service-brake-command.md) and
  [emergency-brake-command](../signals/emergency-brake-command.md).
