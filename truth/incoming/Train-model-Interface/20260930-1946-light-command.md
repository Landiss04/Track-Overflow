# light-command

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Train Controller
**Provenance:** `Train_Model_Backend_Design.pdf` §5.5 (Locked) and Interfaces table, supplied by Kevin Schillinger 2026-09-30; interior/exterior elements asserted by Kevin Schillinger 2026-09-30
**Aliases:** Light Command, lights command, cabin and headlight command
**Last updated:** 2026-09-30

## Definition

Two booleans, `bool[2]`, sent from the Train Controller to the Train Model. They command
the interior lights (element 0) and the exterior lights (element 1).

## Notes

- The Train Controller owns dusk/dawn and tunnel lighting logic. The Train Model applies
  the command and makes no lighting decisions of its own.
- The Train Model reflects the commanded state and reports the actual state back as
  Light State (`bool[2]`), a separate signal with the same element order.
- The source is the Train Model design. The Train Controller owners have not confirmed
  it.

## Supersedes

- Elements: previously named cabin lights and headlights in the design. They are now
  interior (cabin) and exterior (headlight) (Kevin 2026-09-30).
