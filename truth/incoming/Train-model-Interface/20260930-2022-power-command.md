# power-command

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Train Controller
**Provenance:** `Train_Model_Backend_Design.pdf` §1 (Locked) and Interfaces table, supplied by Kevin Schillinger 2026-09-30
**Aliases:** Power Command, commanded power, P_cmd
**Last updated:** 2026-09-30

## Definition

The engine power output, in W, that the Train Controller commands from the Train Model
on every tick.

## Notes

- The Train Model applies the commanded power up to its maximum engine power. Engine
  failure reduces the applied power to zero.
- The source leaves open whether the Train Model limits speed itself or leaves speed
  regulation, including downhill, to the Train Controller. This is pending the
  instructor.
- The source is the Train Model design. The Train Controller owners have not confirmed
  it.
