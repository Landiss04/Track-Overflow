# D022-yard-black-box

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Kevin
**Provenance:** asserted by Kevin Schillinger 2026-10-16
**Aliases:** yard, train yard, yard black box, dispatch from the yard
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16

## Context

Each line's layout joins the yard (`next_blocks` lists `"yard"`, per
`conventions/files-and-paths.md`). No entry said what the yard is or who moves trains
into and out of it.

## Decision

- The yard is a black box with unlimited capacity.
- The CTC Office pulls trains from the yard and sends them back into the yard.

## Consequences

- Open: how a train pulled from the yard comes to exist in the Train Model and the
  Train Controller is not stated. No signal carries a dispatch or a return.
