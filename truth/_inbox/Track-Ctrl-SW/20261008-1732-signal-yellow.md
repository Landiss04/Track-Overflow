## Decision

The software Track Controller signal aspects are Red, Yellow, Green and Super
Green. Yellow replaces Orange in the aspect naming and display. Super Green is
a supported signal aspect.

This decision does not prescribe the conditions under which a PLC program must
request Super Green.
