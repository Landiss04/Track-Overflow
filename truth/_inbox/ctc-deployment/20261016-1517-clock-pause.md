# clock-pause

**Status:** current
**Owner:** CTC Office
**Provenance:** the CTC Office pauses and resumes the shared clock through the central harness, D017 (asserted by Landis 2026-10-01); pause and resume as a CTC Office output, asserted by Landis 2026-10-16; its form (one boolean, starting paused, also readable while no step happens) proposed by Claude on ctc-deployment 2026-10-16, for Landis to confirm
**Aliases:** clock paused, pause, resume, run / pause, clock pause command
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16

## Definition

A boolean sent from the CTC Office to the central harness: true commands the shared
simulation clock to pause, false to run. It is true while the dispatcher has the clock
paused, and false once they resume it. A simulation starts paused, until the dispatcher
runs it.

## Notes

- The control comes from the CTC Office, as for
  [clock-speedup](clock-speedup.md). The central harness pauses and resumes the shared
  clock on it, so every module stops and starts together
  ([D017](../decisions/D017-shared-simulation-clock.md)).
- While the clock is paused no step happens, so the CTC Office cannot report a resume
  as a step's output. The harness also reads the value outside a step, from the CTC
  Office's snapshot.
