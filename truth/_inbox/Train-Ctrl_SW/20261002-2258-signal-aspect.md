# signal-aspect

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Track Model
**Provenance:** asserted by Jonathan Tsang 2026-10-02
**Aliases:** signal aspect, signal light state, traffic signal light state, wayside signal light, signal lights
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

## Definition

The wayside signal light state for the block ahead, sent from the Track Model to the
Train Controller. It takes one of four values: RED, YELLOW, GREEN, or SUPER GREEN.

## Notes

- The Track Model owner has not confirmed this entry.

  beside the Track Signal, or what the Train Controller must do for each aspect.
