# beacon

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Track Model
**Provenance:** `Train_Model_Backend_Design.pdf` §5.7 (Locked) and Interfaces table, supplied by Kevin Schillinger 2026-09-30
**Aliases:** Beacon, station beacon, beacon data
**Last updated:** 2026-09-30

## Definition

Station data broadcast from beacons mounted before and after each station. It has three
fields: station name, platform side (L or R), and whether the station is underground.
The Track Model delivers it to the Train Model, which passes it through to the Train
Controller.

## Notes

- The train receives a beacon only near a station, through its antenna.
- The source leaves two questions open: the serialized payload must fit in 128
  characters but no format is set, and it does not say what the Train Model outputs
  between beacons (nothing, or the last one received).
- The source is the Train Model design. The Track Model and Train Controller owners have
  not confirmed it.
