# D007-station-dwell

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Kevin
**Provenance:** `Train_Model_Backend_Design.pdf` §5.2 (Locked), supplied by Kevin Schillinger 2026-09-30; enforcement by the Train Controller, with the test UI standing in until integration, asserted by Kevin Schillinger 2026-10-06
**Aliases:** dwell time, station dwell, 45 second dwell
**Last updated:** 2026-10-06

## Context

At each station, passengers disembark and board. Every module that plans or times a stop
needs the same value for how long a train stays at a station.

## Decision

Station dwell time is 45 s, fixed. Every station stop lasts 45 s.

The Train Controller enforces the dwell. Until integration the test UI, standing in for
the Train Controller (D010), holds the train at a station for it.

## Consequences

- The dwell does not vary by station or passenger count.
- The Train Model does not enforce the dwell; it stays physics only (D009).
