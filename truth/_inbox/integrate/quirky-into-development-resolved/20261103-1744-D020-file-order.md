# D020-automatic-dispatch-from-schedule

**Status:** current
**Owner:** Landis
**Provenance:** asserted by Landis 2026-10-14 (the CTC Office stops trains at their scheduled stations); trains stop only at the stations they are scheduled at, confirmed by Landis 2026-10-14; the schedule's time zero is the moment it starts, and runs stop at their stations in the schedule's order, asserted by Landis 2026-11-03
**Aliases:** automatic dispatch, auto dispatch, scheduling algorithm, schedule runs, track order, file order, stop order, scheduled stops, schedule time zero, T = 0
**Last updated:** 2026-11-03

## Context

In automatic mode the CTC Office loads and runs a train schedule (REQ-FUNC-007,
REQ-FUNC-010). The schedule, `documents/Project_Information/Schedule v4.xlsx` (as
`utils/schedule_v4.json`), lists each run's timed stops in block-number order.

## Decision

- **Scheduled stations only.** A train stops only at the stations its run is scheduled
  at. A station on its way that is not its next scheduled one, it runs through without
  stopping. A scheduled station is a block the schedule lists for the run that the
  track layout marks as a station, named or not.
- **In the schedule's order.** A run stops at its stations in the order the schedule
  lists them, whatever that costs. From the yard it runs to its first station without
  stopping, then on to each next one in turn, then back to the yard. Where the track
  reaches the next station only after another lap (Green: Edgebrook, 9, after Pioneer,
  2, since the track runs 12 to 1), the run makes that lap. A station the track cannot
  reach from where the run is, it skips.
- **The CTC Office stops the trains.** It sends a train to its next scheduled station
  (its authority ends there), holds it there for the station dwell
  ([D007](D007-station-dwell.md)), then sends it on to the next.
- **Time zero is the moment the schedule starts**: the simulation time when the shared
  clock ([D017](D017-shared-simulation-clock.md)) runs with the schedule loaded. That
  is when the dispatcher presses Run with a schedule loaded, or the moment a schedule
  is loaded while the clock is already running, whatever the simulation time is then.
  A run is due to leave the yard at time zero plus its departure time in the schedule.
  Loading a schedule again starts it again.

## Consequences

- The schedule's per-stop times are not used as given. Each run's departure time is
  used, and its time at each station follows from its route and the dwells before it.
- Runs keep the schedule's headway however late it is loaded.
- Blocks the schedule times that are not stations are not stops.
- Nothing is dispatched or moved on outside automatic mode, or while the dispatcher has
  paused automatic dispatch.

## Notes

- Contradicts the proposed D012 (on `Train-Ctrl_SW`), under which a train in automatic
  mode stops at every station by itself. Under this decision it stops only at its
  scheduled stations, which are where its authority ends.

## Supersedes

- Stop order: previously the order the track reaches the stations from the yard, a
  first approach (Landis 2026-10-14); now the schedule's order (Landis 2026-11-03).
- Time zero: previously the simulation start, 05:00:00; now the moment the schedule
  starts (Landis 2026-11-03).
