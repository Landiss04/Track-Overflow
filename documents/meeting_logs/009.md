## Meeting Notes

A short working-week check-in. The team reminded each other to re-sync module I/O
with their dependencies, to test UIs hard before Thursday, and sorted out how
Landis will present remotely. Two logistics questions were left open pending a
Piazza answer.

### Cross-Module I/O Alignment

The architecture diagram covered interfaces at a high level, but they will shift
as implementation proceeds. Everyone should get together with the owners of the
modules they depend on and confirm the I/O still matches, then keep those
definitions updated rather than letting them drift. Landis needs to do this with
Braden and Harry for his own module.

The broader reminder was to stay on pace: finishing early leaves room to work
through problems. Landis is not especially worried — the team seems to have a good
grasp of what is required — but anyone blocked on someone else's work should chase
it now rather than late in the week.

### UI Testing and Peer Review

UIs are easy to leave subtle bugs in that go unnoticed. Kevin's recommendation is
to have Claude exercise the UI, since it will test every individual element and
cover edge cases the author would not think of. Landis added that a second pair of
human eyes catches different problems — ask a teammate to look over your UI and
flag whatever jumps out immediately. Catching it now is cheaper than hitting it
during the demo.

### Thursday Presentation Logistics

Landis is presenting over Zoom on Thursday. The consensus is the presentation is
the UI itself, with no slides needed — and juggling slides alongside a live UI over
Zoom would only make it harder. Kevin offered to drive Landis's UI in the room
while Landis narrated, and Ivan suggested someone screen share it back to him, but
Landis decided he will just screen share it himself since it is not that
complicated.

Whether every module has to run on one machine is unresolved. Kevin assumes each
person can present from their own laptop, but his laptop does not play nicely with
the room's projector and Jonathan has no HDMI adapter. Ivan's position is that the
team should at minimum get all modules running on a single computer as a fallback,
and anyone who would rather present from their own machine still can. He is adding
the question to his Piazza post.
